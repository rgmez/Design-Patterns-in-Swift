# Choose Swift Design Pattern

Assess observable design pressure in an iOS, macOS, or server-side Swift
feature and produce one reviewable recommendation. Treat a direct Swift or
platform solution as a successful outcome, and do not introduce abstraction
only to anticipate hypothetical growth.

This initial entry point establishes the skill's scope. The evidence-based
decision workflow and its progressive catalogue references are intentionally
added in the next implementation milestones. Until then, state when the
supplied evidence is insufficient instead of inventing requirements.
